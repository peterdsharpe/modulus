2026-09-09 — Program start: from the audit’s agenda to preregistered experiments

Setting. The companion review of the ISLA record (research/cross_dataset_generalization/, untracked; rendered report at its _book/index.html) audited the repository, thirty-one primary papers and five analytic controls, and concluded that the record does not establish a cross-dataset accuracy advantage for ISLA; with the freestream input matched, GeoTransolver and Transolver are more accurate at both completed HiLiftAeroML rungs, and on the DrivAerML → SHIFT-SUV fastback transfer ISLA’s source-only error is 0.99 against GeoTransolver’s 0.59, both falling to 0.11–0.13 when the estate family joins training. The review proposed four directions (rich computational support with independent queries; topology- and conditioning-aware boundary responses; local flux responses with numerical global coupling; transferred correction spaces), a physical-input prerequisite, and a nonlinear extension.

Decision. This program runs in a worktree parallel to the main ISLA session (which owns the program book and its cluster lanes) and keeps in sync with it at milestones. Order of work, as the review recommended: controls first (C1 pressure-offset versus spatial error, eval-only on saved predictions; C2 source-only normalization audit; C3 a paired physical-condition control with exact labels), then in parallel the computational-support study (D1, which needs training lanes and therefore node budget from the main program) and the three nonlearned gates (D2 boundary-response rank and basis transfer on 2D Laplace; D3 local-response composition on 2D diffusion ports; D4 solver correction-space transfer on 2D finite-element systems), each run by a separate agent in its own worktree with a preregistration written before its first run. A literature chapter (L) asks what classical solvers do that surrogates do not.

Heilmeier answers, compressed. We want field and force predictions on geometries and families outside the training distribution without a new labeled dataset. Today’s surrogates fit their family and fail outside it, and the largest reported advantages in this program turned out to be protocol artifacts. The new element is not an architecture but a set of gates: each proposed mechanism has a cheap nonlearned test that can reject it before training. If it works, the payoff is a surrogate whose failure modes are understood and whose accuracy is stated with its cost. The risks are that no mechanism passes its gate (a useful negative), or that a passing gate does not carry to turbulent 3D data. Cost: CPU studies today; one four-lane training study when budget allows.

2026-09-09 — Preregistered: C1, pressure-offset versus spatial error on the fastback transfer

Setting. DrivAerML (static wheels, fixed freestream, OpenFOAM hybrid RANS–LES) and SHIFT-SUV (rolling floor, rotating wheels, a different freestream speed and a different detached-eddy formulation) are different label-generating conventions on smooth single-body car families. Pressure is nondimensionalized by each dataset’s own metadata (q_∞) and not shifted, so a difference in the two datasets’ pressure reference would appear in a zero-shot prediction as a per-case constant, and a constant is exactly what makes a relative L2 error approach 1.0 when the predicted field is otherwise sensible. ISLA’s source-only fastback error is 0.99; GeoTransolver’s 0.59.

Instrument (eval-only). From the saved zero-shot predictions (smoke_fastback/{mt2_v3c,gt_fixed,mt2_multi,gt_multi}_seed{42,43,44}, 99 fastback cases) and the in-family DrivAerML evaluations of the same checkpoints, with cell areas recomputed from the saved triangles and cell normals from the saved mesh: per case and arm (seed-mean predictions), the area-weighted mean error (the offset), the centered remainder, the share of area-weighted squared error carried by the offset, the relative L2 error of the full and of the offset-removed error against the centered true field, the surface closedness |Σ a n| / Σ a, and the relative error of the integrated pressure force with and without the offset. Script: studies/controls/pressure_offset_diagnostic.py.

Bars, in readout units. For the two source-only fastback arms: offset-dominated if the constant carries ≥ 50% of the area-weighted squared error (median over cases) and the offset-removed relative L2 is at most 0.6 of the full one; spatial if the constant carries ≤ 20%; between, mixed. The in-family arms must show an offset share ≤ 10%, or the instrument is not measuring a transfer effect. Force: on surfaces with closedness ≤ 0.05 the force error with and without the offset must agree within the offset’s share, or the normals are inconsistent and the force reading is withdrawn.

What each outcome changes. Offset-dominated: the historical transfer gap is a label-convention question before it is an architecture question; C3 (paired physical conditions) becomes the next control, and any transfer claim must state the pressure reference. Spatial: the gap is a genuine field error; the offset hypothesis is closed and D1/D2 proceed on their own merits. Either way the readout is descriptive: target labels are used only to diagnose, never to correct predictions.

2026-09-09 — Verdict: C1 spatial — the zero-shot fastback error is a field error, not a pressure-reference offset (offset share 1.5–2.6% for ISLA, 3.0–3.2% for GeoTransolver)

Verdict: spatial, for both architectures. The constant carries 1.5% (ISLA) and 3.0% (GeoTransolver) of the source-only squared error by the median over cases (2.6% and 3.2% by the mean), far below the 20% floor of the “spatial” band; removing it changes the relative error by under 1%. The in-family arms pass the instrument check (offset share under 1%). The offsets that do exist are small and of the same sign for both models (+0.14 of the true field’s RMS), which is consistent with a modest difference of pressure reference between the datasets but explains none of the gap. The transfer failure is a spatial field error, and it is also a force error: 1.6x (ISLA) and 1.4x (GeoTransolver) of the true force magnitude against 0.04 in-family, falling to 0.28 and 0.20 when the estate family joins training.

What it changes. The pressure-reference hypothesis is closed. The compound physical-condition mismatch between the datasets (ground and wheel motion, freestream speed, solver formulation) remains the leading explanation that is not about architecture, and C3 tests its logic on an exactly specified problem. Any transfer claim in this program states the force error alongside the field error, since a field error of 0.9 is a force error above 100%.

2026-09-09 — Verdict: D4 stops — a free geometric coarse grid beats a frozen source-fitted correction space on every held-out elliptic system

The solver-space probe (studies/solver_spaces/, preregistered in its PREREG.md before the run, 47 s on one CPU) compared a frozen proper orthogonal decomposition of smoother-resistant source errors against equal-dimension classical coarse spaces on 2D finite-element diffusion systems with holes, under within-family, topology and coefficient shifts. The frozen space left 0.75–0.96 of the slow-mode error where the geometric coarse grid left 0.47–0.73 (ratios 0.62–0.76 against bars of 2.0 and 1.5), and complete solves with it cost 19–72% more work than with the classical space; the per-target oracle decomposition lost to the grid as well. The mechanism is that solution snapshots span the right-hand sides seen at the source geometries, not the operator’s slow subspace, which for an elliptic operator under a point smoother is generic smoothness and comes free. The convection–diffusion variant was uninformative at mesh Péclet 0.5 and is recorded as not measured. Chapter 5  Transferring a solver correction space has the tables; the direction’s only remaining homes are operator classes where classical coarse spaces fail, and learned smoothers or prolongations on a classical hierarchy, both behind prerequisite gates (6  What classical solvers do that surrogates do not).

2026-09-09 — C2, source-only normalization: audited from the configurations, one item flagged

Both transfer dataset configurations (drivaer_ml_surface.yaml, shift_suv_fastback_surface.yaml) nondimensionalize pressure and wall shear by each case’s own metadata (dynamic pressure) with no shift, and normalize wall shear by a declared per-dataset constant (standard deviation 0.00313 for DrivAerML, 0.00183 for SHIFT-SUV; zero mean). Inference denormalizes predictions with the statistics saved at training time (source-side), and the relative L2 metric is invariant to a common scale, so the differing wall shear constants do not bias the reported relative errors; they would bias any absolute or mixed-field loss. Pressure carries no normalization constant beyond the physical nondimensionalization, so the C1 result is not a normalization artifact either. Flagged: the wall-shear constant is target-side in the target configuration (a statistic of SHIFT-SUV chosen when its config was written); for a zero-shot protocol the target configuration should carry the source’s constant, which changes nothing for relative metrics and is recorded here as a protocol rule. Verdict: normalizations are source-only in effect for the reported metrics; one declared-constant hygiene item.

2026-09-09 — Verdict: D3 not worth a learner as proposed — smooth-mode local errors are amplified 4–30x by the interface solve and grids grow superlinearly; the surviving form is a hybrid with exact low modes

The composition gate (studies/composition/, preregistered in its PREREG.md, 1,064 s on one CPU) assembled 2D finite-element diffusion components with identical straight ports into chains and grids of 2–16 components and perturbed every local response by a relative Frobenius error ε. Exact composition is exact (composition law 2 × 10⁻¹⁶, interface solve 5 × 10⁻¹⁵). At ε = 10⁻² the median global energy error is 1.7% for two components, 7% for chains of eight, 33% for grids of eight and 34% for grids of sixteen (a 6.2x growth from four to sixteen, faster than linear), missing the 5% bar; errors concentrated on rough port modes are damped 20–100x while errors on smooth modes are amplified 4–30x and set the global error, since the smooth small-eigenvalue part of a response carries the load (truncating a response below 63 of 64 modes fails catastrophically). Transfer-optimal port modes transfer to new shapes but need 13–15 of 15 modes for 1%; proper-orthogonal modes are shape-specific; at rank 4 both leave 16–27% error, missing the reduction bar. The interface solve is cheaper than the monolithic solve only up to four components, and a partition-of-unity coarse space does not help the iteration count. The narrow-channel family did not place a barrier at a port, so the near-field claim is untested by this instrument. Verdict against the bars: not worth a learner as proposed. What survives is a hybrid solver (exact smooth low-mode response, learned rough remainder), with a split-response test and a port-adjacent barrier family as its next nonlearned gates, not launched here. Chapter 4  Local-response composition has the tables; figures studies/composition/fig_*.png.

2026-09-09 — D1 launched: six lanes for computational support with a depth-matched passive readout

What was built (merged from the D1 track, commit 834bd779e on this branch). ISLA(support_tokens=True, query_independent=True, n_query_scalars=1, query_mass="source_total", n_decoder_layers=K): the forward takes support_points / support_normals / support_scalars, which join the encoder as interacting tokens with the same invariant seeds, a token-type offset and a learned total mass equal to a learned fraction of the total source measure; requested query_points / query_normals / query_scalars are decoded passively through K read blocks that see the final slice states, the anchors and a normalized log-space kernel readout of all encoder tokens. Ten contract tests pass (exact query independence with the support fixed, at 1e-12, alone or with 500 foreign companions; SE(3) covariance; drive degree one; measure-scale and source-refinement invariance; gauge scale equivariance; geo_checkpoint exactness; every parameter receives gradients); the existing ISLA suites (63) pass. 2026-09-09 — Finding while building D1: the legacy passive kernel readout clamps the kernel mass, so far-from-surface queries scale with the absolute source weights

In physicsnemo/experimental/nn/isla/model.py, the passive decoder’s local token readout _kernel_readout(q_r, src_r, src_h, src_w, rho, eps) (called from _ReadBlock.forward when src_h is given, with rho = local_readout_rho, default 0.02 in gauge-normalized coordinates) computes k = exp(-d²/ρ²) · w and divides by mass = k.sum(-1).clamp_min(eps). For a query farther than a few ρ from every source point the true mass is below eps = 1e-12, the clamp replaces it, and the readout becomes Σ k h / eps, which scales with the absolute source weights instead of being a weighted mean: in the D1 track’s test a 4.2x rescale of the source weights moved the legacy passive output by 0.16 in relative norm at such queries. With ρ = 0.02 and interior queries whose median wall distance is about 5 mm on a 4 m car (below ρ in gauge units) most queries are inside the kernel’s reach, but the far tail of the interior sample is not, so the legacy passive arm’s 2.6x pressure deficit (program book, interior chapter) includes a contribution from this readout regime that the arm’s measure-scale contract test did not exercise. The legacy path is left bitwise unchanged so trained checkpoints reproduce; the support path uses an exactly normalized log-space form (softmax over sources with logits −d²/ρ² + log w), identical where the mass is not tiny. The PASSIVE-12 arm, which uses the normalized readout with 12 read blocks and the SDF scalar, bounds how much of the 2.6x was readout and depth rather than passivity; the main session has asked for this note to attach a caveat to that figure once the arm reports.

Launch acceptance (addendum). SUPPORT-12 steps at 0.52 s and 10.8 GB per GPU and SUPPORT-4 at 0.46 s and 7.1 GB (the QT+SDF reference: 0.28 s, 6.3 GB), both at epoch 3–4 within the first hour; the cost readout is therefore already known to be 1.6–1.9x the reference’s step time. The two PASSIVE-12 chains blocked at model construction: with support_tokens=false the model rejects query_mass=source_total (the option needs interacting query or support tokens), so the override model.query_mass=geometric_mean was added to that arm (inert without interacting tokens), the block markers were cleared and the chains resubmitted (jobs 691370/691371). The preregistration is unchanged by this: the arm’s definition never involved a query mass.
